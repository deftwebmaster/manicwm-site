CrossRoads Decorative Pavement / Client project · In progress

Making the workspeak for the business.

CrossRoads Decorative Pavement creates distinctive streets, crosswalks, plazas, and pavement artwork. Working directly with Paul Black, I’m redesigning the company’s website to give that work a clearer, more substantial presentation.

The brief

Let prospective customers see the craft.

The project photography provides the evidence. The website’s job is to organize it, explain the services behind it, and make the next conversation easy to start.

I designed and developed the new site and its features, bringing the service information, project collection, company background, and contact paths into a connected experience.

The features

More ways to explore the work.

A filterable gallery lets visitors focus on the kinds of projects that interest them. Larger photo previews give the details room to be seen, while before-and-after sliders show the transformation in place.

Clear service information and direct contact paths help prospective customers understand what CrossRoads does and explore relevant examples before getting in touch.

  • A filterable project gallery with enlarged photo viewing.
  • Interactive before-and-after comparisons.
  • A multi-page website with clear service and company information.
  • Direct calling and contact paths throughout the experience.

Current status · In progress

The finishing details.

Paul and I are finalizing the redesign. He’s pleased with the work so far, and the focus is now on bringing the remaining details together.
