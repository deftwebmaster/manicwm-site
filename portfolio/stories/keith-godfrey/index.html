Keith A Godfrey / Client project · Completed

A portfolio built forthe next opportunity.

Keith needed a dedicated home for his photography—a portfolio he could confidently share with prospective employers. I designed and developed the website from the ground up, giving his photographs room to speak.

The brief

Make the work easy to share.

Keith already had the work. He needed a presentation that could introduce him, show the range of his photography, and give a prospective employer a clear way to explore it.

The site needed to feel like his portfolio from the first screen, with the photographs carrying the story.

The direction

An editorial frame for the photographs.

Bold typography introduces the photographer. A restrained palette and varied image layouts carry visitors through portraits, still life, and nature studies.

The balance is deliberate: give the website its own character while keeping attention on Keith’s images. Generous spacing, short captions, and a clear contact path make the collection feel considered and personal.

  • A complete website designed and developed from the ground up.
  • A collection presented with rhythm and room for individual photographs.
  • A layout that brings the same visual direction to desktop and mobile.

The work, wherever it’s opened

A portfolio that travels with him.

The mobile presentation keeps the introduction direct and gives the photographs the width of the screen. The same identity carries through, with navigation focused on the work and getting in touch.

The outcome

A new portfolio.
A new opportunity.

Keith used the finished website in his job search and landed a job. He’s very happy with the site and the way it presents his photography.
