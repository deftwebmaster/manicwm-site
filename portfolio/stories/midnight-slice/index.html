Midnight Slice / Self-directed · Fictional concept

One fictional pizza shop.A complete visual world.

Midnight Slice began as a fictional late-night pizza concept and grew into a coordinated identity, menu, promotional artwork, and website. The menu became the centerpiece.

The idea

Give pizza after dark its own identity.

I built the visual direction around the energy of a late-night pizza shop: neon color, bold typography, expressive food imagery, and a brand that feels at home on a storefront, a printed menu, or a phone screen.

The goal was a world of connected pieces. The mark, food names, menu, promotional artwork, and website all needed to feel like they came from the same place.

The piece I’m especially proud of

The menu carries
the personality.

The challenge was to bring all that energy onto the page while keeping categories, signature items, prices, and specials easy to navigate.

It’s where the concept becomes something you can imagine using: choosing a slice, finding a special, and getting a feel for the place before you’ve even walked in.

Across the pieces

The same voice, in different formats.

The campaign artwork turns the volume up. The website gives the identity a place to live, extending the menu and promotional direction into an example restaurant experience.

The project shows how I think across formats: carrying an idea from its name and mark into the pieces a business would actually put in front of people.

  • Logo and visual direction for the fictional brand.
  • A detailed restaurant menu and coordinated promotional artwork.
  • A website that extends the same identity into an interactive example.

The finished concept

A brand with somewhere to go.

Midnight Slice is a self-directed design project. Its result is a coordinated collection of identity, print, campaign, and website work that makes the fictional shop feel like a place you could visit.
