About Midnight Slice

Fresh dough. Fast pickup. Chaotic-good pizza energy.

Midnight Slice started as the tiny shop everyone found after concerts, late shifts, and group chats that refused to go home. We make dough every morning, fire pies until late, and keep the counter moving when the rest of the block goes quiet.

The vibe is neon, the sauce is bright, and every order is built to travel well from our oven to your couch, curb, studio, or sidewalk table.
