FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before booking.

Do you charge a diagnostic fee?

Yes. The fee covers a technician visit and system diagnosis. If the customer approves the repair, the fee can be credited toward the work.

Can you repair older systems?

Alpine services all major residential brands and can source many legacy parts. If replacement is the smarter option, the technician explains why.

What counts as an emergency?

No cooling during extreme heat, no heat during a cold snap, electrical smells, refrigerant leaks, and total system failure qualify for emergency dispatch.
