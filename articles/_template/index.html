FAQ

Should every article have a short answer?

Yes. It helps readers immediately and gives the page a clear search-intent match.

How many sections should an article have?

As many as the topic needs, but fewer than the busy version. Useful, calm, complete.

Where does the sales pitch go?

After the advice has earned it. Keep the CTA present but quiet.
