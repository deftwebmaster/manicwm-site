Entry 06 — Imperial Maritime Privateering

BLACKTIDE
PRIVATEERING
CO.

"Fortune Favors The Ruthless."

Blacktide Privateering Co. began as a sanctioned naval contractor protecting imperial trade routes through contested waters. Within decades, its fleets controlled shipping lanes so completely that governments became dependent on the very organization they once hired. Now Blacktide exists beyond nation and crown — a storm-born commercial empire enforcing maritime order through fear, gold, and cannon fire. Every crossing costs. Blacktide simply decides how much.

Entry 07 — Luxury Orbital Hospitality

HELIOS
RING
RESORT

"Vacation Beyond Gravity."

Helios Ring Resort transformed orbital habitation into the highest form of luxury. Built above one of the most visually celebrated worlds in civilized space, the rotating megastructure houses panoramic sky lounges, luxury suites, orbital gardens, fine dining districts, private observation decks, and elite transit terminals for guests arriving from across the stars. For many, visiting Helios is less a vacation than a declaration: that they have finally escaped gravity itself.
