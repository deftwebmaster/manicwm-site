Before we say hello

A few questions.
Clear answers.

Can you help if I don’t have a website yet?

Yes. Tell me about your business and what the website needs to do. We can work out an appropriate starting point.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Share the address and what you’d like to change. I’ll review the details before recommending an approach.

Can you help with a logo or graphic design too?

Yes. Logo, identity, and graphic design can support a website project or be discussed separately. Mention what you need in your inquiry.

Do I need all my content ready?

No. Start with what you have. We’ll discuss what content and assets are needed, and agree on who will provide them as part of the scope.

How much will my website cost?

That depends on the pages, content, design, and functionality involved. I’ll explain the scope and quote before paid work begins.

How long will it take?

Timing depends on the scope, content, feedback, and availability. We’ll discuss a realistic schedule before the project begins.

What if I only need a small fix?

Small fixes and one-off updates are welcome. Visit my website help page or describe what you need in your inquiry.
