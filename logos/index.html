A few questions.
Clear answers.

Can you refresh my existing logo?

Yes. Send a link to what you have and explain what needs to change. I’ll review it before recommending a scope.

Can I hire you just for a logo?

Yes. Logo work can be a standalone project or part of a website and graphics project.

What files will I receive?

Logo projects include transparent PNGs, one-color versions, print exports, color and typography guidance, and upscaling where needed. Your quote confirms the versions, sizes, and formats for your project.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Limited revision rounds are included. We’ll agree on the revision allowance and the work covered in your quote before paid work begins.

How much does a logo cost?

That depends on the work involved. I’ll explain the proposed scope and price before paid work begins.

Do I need a finished idea?

No. A little about the business and a few references you like are a useful starting point. We can figure out the direction together.
